# Nexa Solutions Showcase

Create a premium modern business website demo for a fictional company called "Nexa Business Solutions".

This is a portfolio/demo website that I will show to potential clients, so make it look like a professionally designed real business website, not like an AI-generated template.

Design:

- Clean, premium and modern

- White/off-white background

- Navy blue, soft blue and subtle accent colors

- Excellent typography

- Minimal shadows

- Elegant spacing

- Professional corporate feel

- Fully responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop

- Avoid excessive gradients, animations and flashy effects

Pages:

1. Home

2. About Us

3. Services

4. Projects/Portfolio

5. Why Choose Us

6. Testimonials

7. FAQ

8. Contact

Home page:

- Strong hero section

- Heading: "Helping Businesses Grow in a Digital World"

- Subheading explaining the company

- CTA buttons: "Get Started" and "Contact Us"

- Professional business visual

- Services preview

- Why choose us

- Featured projects

- Testimonials

- CTA section

- Footer

Services:

- Business Consulting

- Digital Solutions

- Marketing Support

- Brand Strategy

- Website Solutions

- Business Growth

Contact:

- Contact form

- Phone

- Email

- Address

- WhatsApp button

- Google Maps section

Use fictional demo information only and clearly keep the content generic enough to replace later.

Make every button, navigation item and form visually functional.

Add subtle hover effects and smooth transitions.

The final website should look like something a real small/medium business could purchase.
